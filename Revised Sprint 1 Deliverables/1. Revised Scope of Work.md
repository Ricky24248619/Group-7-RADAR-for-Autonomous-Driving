## 1. Why this revision exists

Four things changed between 12 August and 5 September. None were within the team's
control, and together they made the original delivery structure unworkable for Sprint 2.

**1. The Scope of Work was never approved.** It was submitted on 12 August. The client
replied once, on 11 August, and has not responded since. We have continued without
approval rather than stall, and have drafted findings and a specific request. The
repository does not establish that those drafts were sent.

**2. Kaya remains inaccessible.** The application requires a Principal Investigator,
a data management plan, ORCIDs, a linked repository and an IRDS share. Adrian agreed to
act as PI, which was the part we could not supply ourselves, but the remaining paperwork
needs his involvement. The team still owns its ORCIDs, repository link and preparation
of the information needed for the application; the remaining responsibilities must be
confirmed rather than assigned entirely to the client.

**3. STONE was dropped on 29 August.** It was the only off-road dataset we found with
annotated 4D imaging radar, and therefore the only candidate that could have joined the
off-road and radar halves of the project. It is a single 346 GB download with no sample
split, contains no toolkit in its repository, and its maintainers have not answered a
GitHub issue since March. Recorded in decision **D-06**.

**4. We reorganised by dataset rather than by epic.** Six people each pursuing a
separate research epic across three unfamiliar datasets produced very little in Sprint
1. Three pairs, one dataset each, produced substantially more in three weeks.

## 2. Initiative — unchanged

Produce an evidence-based comparison of RADAR, LiDAR and camera perception for
autonomous trucking, supported by a reproducible experimental record, and hand over a
documented body of work a future team can extend.

## 4. Revised delivery structure

### 4.1 Three dataset pairs

For Sprint 2 the team works in pairs, one dataset each. Each dataset answers a different
question; they are instruments, not competing candidates.

| Pair | Dataset | Question it answers | Task type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damien Zhang, Ricky Yuen | **GOOSE** (ICRA 2024) | Can we distinguish drivable ground from obstacles, off-road? | Semantic segmentation |
| Aiden Blampain, Fatima Sher | **MAN TruckScenes** (NeurIPS 2024) | Radar vs LiDAR vs camera, like-for-like on shared ground truth | 3D detection |
| Fariya Zehrin, Kelsey Chen | **TruckDrive** (CVPR 2026) | Does perception degrade beyond ~150 m? | 3D detection, long range |

### 4.2 Why pairs, and what returns to epics

Pairing was adopted because Sprint 1's structure spread six people thinly across
unfamiliar territory. Working in pairs on one dataset allowed each pair to reach a
working environment, real measurements and reproducible evidence.

**The epics are deferred, not abandoned.** Resuming them in Sprint 3 is proposed for
the team checkpoint. The mapping is preserved:

| Epic | Owner | Sprint 2 contribution |
|---|---|---|
| A — Simulation base and results presentation | Fariya | TruckDrive exploration; Autoware deferred |
| B — Fundamentals, off-road domain, benchmarking infrastructure | Damien | GOOSE survey; results store and schema (DZ-3, DZ-4) delivered |
| C — 4D RADAR detection and documentation | Fatima | TruckScenes radar/LiDAR visualisation in clear, snow and rain |
| D — Dataset exploration and sensor visualisation | Kelsey | TruckDrive viewer validated; 24 mini scenes prepared |
| E — Industry and technology review | Aiden | TruckScenes devkit; industry review not started |
| F — Evaluation framework, reproducibility, coordination | Ricky | Metric definitions, experiment logs, first model run |

### 4.3 Shared infrastructure

Three pairs producing results in parallel is how three incompatible formats get created.
Two shared components prevent it, both delivered in Sprint 2:

- **`docs/metrics-definitions.md`** — every metric defined before it is reported, with
  rules on what mIoU averages over and why segmentation and detection metrics never
  share a table.
- **`results/`** — one JSON record per result, schema-validated. `dataset`,
  `sensor_configuration` and `annotation_schema` can never be blank, which enforces
  decision D-01 at the data layer. Failed runs are first-class records.

## 5. Revised scope boundaries

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| Dataset acquisition, devkit loading, sensor visualisation across all three datasets | Training a model from scratch |
| Per-dataset characterisation with measured statistics | Production deployment or vehicle integration |
| A shared, validated results store and metric definitions | Real-world or on-vehicle testing |
| Running existing open-source models where hardware permits | Any work requiring Kaya, until access is granted |
| Reproducible environments across macOS and Windows | STONE (dropped, D-06) |
| Documentation, decision log, handbook, handover material | Autoware / ROS 2 (deferred to Sprint 3, D-07) |
| Negative results, recorded with evidence | Novel algorithm development |

## 6. Constraints

Carried from Sprint 1, with what changed:

- **CITS3200 is one of four units per member.** Unchanged.
- **Compute.** Partly resolved. Risk R-08 assumed no member had a discrete NVIDIA GPU;
  an audit on 27 August found a **GTX 1660, 6 GiB**, which is the team's only CUDA
  machine. Bounded inference works with the documented runtime patch, FP32,
  FlashAttention off, patch sizes 64 and one augmentation. The ten-frame loop-body
  mean extrapolates to **~5 hours**, excluding data loading; full-split resource use
  remains unmeasured. Further sustained local work requires Ricky's explicit approval.
- **Dataset access differs by dataset.** GOOSE is open; TruckScenes is open via AWS;
  TruckDrive requires accepting a non-commercial licence.
- **No tokens or credits for AI tooling.** Costs are borne by members.
- **Client availability is now a material constraint** and is recorded as risk R-28.

## 7. Recorded decisions

D-01 to D-05 are carried forward from Sprint 1. D-06 to D-09 are recorded in
`decision-log.md` with their evidence and confirmation status; client approval or
notification is not inferred from the existence of a draft note.

**D-06 — STONE dropped.** 29 August. The only off-road dataset found with annotated 4D
imaging radar, but a 346 GB monolithic download with no sample split, no devkit in its
repository, and maintainers unresponsive since March. Storage alone was necessary but
not sufficient; the absent toolkit was also a blocker. Client notification is not
verified by the cited draft note.

**D-07 — Proposed Autoware deferral to Sprint 3.** Confirm the schedule at the team
checkpoint. It targets Ubuntu with ROS 2 and no team
member currently runs Linux natively. GOOSE ships ROS bags natively, making it the
cheapest entry point when the strand resumes — but they are **ROS 1**, so conversion is
still required.

**D-08 — Delivery reorganised by dataset for Sprint 2.** Returning to epics in Sprint 3
is proposed for confirmation. See §4.2 and the dataset PRs cited in D-08; no
sent-message evidence of this scope change is recorded.

**D-09 — Work proceeds without client approval.** Rather than stall, the team continued
under the Sprint 1 scope as written and documented variance. A client note with
findings and a specific request is drafted; notification remains unverified.

## 8. What we delivered in Sprint 2

Evidence is in the team repository and its pull requests. GOOSE's bounded run (#17)
and TruckDrive's exploration (#23) are merged. TruckScenes' exploration (#21) remains
open; its evidence is reviewable but is not yet integrated into `main`.

**All three datasets are loading, visualised and characterised.**

| Dataset | Measured | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| GOOSE | 961 frames, 174,891,807 labelled points; 62.9% within 25 m, 3.8% beyond 100 m | `docs/dataset-surveys/goose.md`, `dataset-statistics.md` |
| TruckDrive | 24 mini scenes; 12,502 radar frames; 526,148 3D boxes; 27 classes; **6,746 radar detections at ≥150 m** | `TruckDrive - Kelsey/` |
| TruckScenes | Mini split characterised; LiDAR and radar visualised in clear, snow and rain | `docs/dataset-surveys/truckscenes.md`, `docs/evidence/truckscenes/` |

**Shared infrastructure delivered:** results store with schema validation; metric
definitions; experiment log; survey template; cross-platform environment documentation;
a project handbook; and a session guard enforcing file ownership between parallel
contributors.

**First model run:** PTv3 executed on GOOSE validation data on the team's GTX 1660 in
FP32 at ~19 s/frame. Stopped deliberately after 10 of 961 frames. Recorded as
diagnostics, not benchmark metrics, because a partial run is not a benchmark.
