## 5. Assessment approach

- Per-dataset tests (§2) are assessed at the sprint boundary by a member from a
  different pair.
- Project-level criteria are assessed at the Sprint 3 handover. **P-5** has supporting
  cross-platform evidence within the team; its outside-team check is still required.
- A test that cannot be met is recorded with the reason rather than quietly dropped, and
  the reason is carried into the final report.
- **A test blocked by an external dependency is recorded as blocked, not failed.** P-1
  is blocked on client availability; that is a failed dependency, not a team outcome.
