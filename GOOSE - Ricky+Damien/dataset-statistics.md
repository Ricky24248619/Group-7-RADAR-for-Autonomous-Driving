## Interpretation boundaries

- These numbers describe ground-truth label prevalence, not model quality.
- A class absent from a scenario must not be silently treated as IoU zero; the metric definition must state the included class set.
- Scenario composition is a major confound. Weather and terrain cannot be separated by comparing the rain and sunny recordings without route matching.
- Sparse points beyond 150 m do not make GOOSE a long-range benchmark.
